## 9. First month: post prompts

These are prompts, not drafts. Each needs one real detail from Denise before it gets written.

**Week 1: introduce the shift**
1. *Leadership:* the generations of technology she's worked through and what stayed constant. `[DT: two or three earlier technologies she led through]`
2. *Opportunity:* why "options" is the word she keeps coming back to for career security. `[DT: a moment options mattered in her own career]`
3. *Possibility:* a short screen demo of an AI tool doing a real task she does. `[DT: which task]`

**Week 2: what she teaches**
4. *Opportunity:* what her graduate students worry about most and what she tells them. `[DT: the real concern]`
5. *Leadership:* the hardest people decision in a transformation, unattributed. `[DT: situation]`
6. *Possibility:* a creative experiment outside work. `[DT: project]`

**Week 3: many roles**
7. *Opportunity:* holding several roles at once, and how technology makes room for it. `[DT: concrete example from a real week]`
8. *Leadership:* a live news story on AI and jobs, with her practitioner read. Pick from that week's news.
9. *Possibility:* before and after on a task that used to take her hours. `[DT: task and real times]`

**Week 4: launch *More Possible***
10. Newsletter issue 1: "What I mean by more possible." Built from the core belief and bio.
11. *Leadership:* what leaders owe people during AI adoption. `[DT: one experience]`
12. *Possibility:* demo that ties to the newsletter.

The `dt-linkedin` skill handles drafting from here: it checks the news, enforces the voice rules, and keeps drafts in chat until approved.

## 10. What to track (first 90 days)

- Followers gained per week, and the share who match the lead audience (professionals, especially women navigating career change).
- Which pillar earns the most saves and comments. Saves are the clearest signal of usefulness.
- Newsletter subscribers.
- Profile views from people with speaking-related titles (event, program, conference).

At 90 days, use these to confirm or adjust the primary audience in the brand guide.
